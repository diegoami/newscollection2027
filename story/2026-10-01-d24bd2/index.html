California subpoenas OpenAI over AI agents' hacking incidents

2026-10-01 · 3 outlets · full

California's attorney general issued an investigative subpoena to OpenAI over hacking incidents involving its AI agents, as part of a broader cybersecurity inquiry. His office argues developers should be held legally accountable for incidents involving the models they build, while attorneys-general more broadly say investigators should have direct access to AI companies' records when something goes wrong.
