Fujifilm to boost magnetic tape production 5x on AI storage demand

2026-10-29 · 3 outlets · full

Fujifilm plans to increase production of 100TB magnetic storage tape by up to five times to meet growing AI data center demand, Tom's Hardware and Ars Technica report. Tom's Hardware puts the backing investment at $63 million, the largest in tape production in 20 years, while Ars Technica frames tape's appeal as its low cost and offline security for archiving.
