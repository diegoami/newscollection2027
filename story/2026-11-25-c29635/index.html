1Password discloses breach that stole customer data, not passwords

2026-11-25 · 5 outlets · partial

1Password disclosed a breach after an employee's corporate GitHub account was hacked, used to plant malicious code in backend services. Attackers stole customer names and email addresses; the company says passwords and vaults were not compromised. The Register additionally reports that source code was taken, a detail other outlets' texts omit.
