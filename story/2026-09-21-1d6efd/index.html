Meta Patches Muse AI Zero-Day Flaw After Researcher Discovery

2026-09-21 · 3 outlets · partial

Meta issued a patch for a zero-day vulnerability in its Muse AI app that could let local attackers redirect voice data or take control of the agent, per The Register, The Verge and Wired. The Verge says security researcher Patrick Wardle found the bug. Outlets differ on how far the flaw's reach was.
