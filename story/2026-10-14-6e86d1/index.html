Global timekeepers vote to retire the leap second, adding a leap hour instead

2026-10-14 · 5 outlets · conflicting

International timekeeping officials voted to abolish the leap second, the periodic adjustment used to keep clocks aligned with Earth's rotation, replacing it in the future with an occasional leap hour. Officials say the change avoids the kind of computer-system disruption associated with the Y2K bug. Outlets differ on how long until a leap hour adjustment is actually needed, with estimates ranging from several centuries to thousands of years.
