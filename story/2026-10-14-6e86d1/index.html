BIPM votes to scrap the leap second in favor of a leap hour

2026-10-14 · 5 outlets · partial

International timekeepers voted to abolish the leap second, replacing it with an occasional leap hour adjustment instead. The change aims to prevent the kind of computer and IoT disruptions seen with Y2K-style bugs caused by frequent small time corrections. Outlets differ on how soon a leap hour would actually be needed, with estimates ranging from centuries to thousands of years.
