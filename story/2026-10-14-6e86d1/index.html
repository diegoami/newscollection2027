International timekeepers vote to abolish the leap second, adopting a future leap hour

2026-10-14 · 4 outlets · full

After years of debate, the world's official timekeepers voted to scrap the leap second, the periodic adjustment added to keep clocks aligned with Earth's rotation, partly to avoid infrastructure problems akin to the Y2K bug. It will eventually be replaced by a leap hour, though that adjustment is not expected to be needed for thousands of years.
