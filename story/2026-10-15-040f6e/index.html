New York representative proposes bill guaranteeing video game ownership rights

2026-10-15 · 3 outlets · full

A New York congressman introduced a bill to guarantee video game buyers true ownership rather than just a license, following Pennsylvania's lead by requiring publishers to offer an offline mode and the ability to transfer games. Reports note the proposal still faces significant technical and legislative hurdles.
