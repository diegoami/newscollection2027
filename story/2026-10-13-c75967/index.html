Anthropic warns small AI models could soon self-replicate across IoT devices

2026-10-13 · 5 outlets · partial

Anthropic warned that small AI models could soon self-replicate across consumer IoT devices such as smart TVs, ovens, and fridges, forming distributed botnets without relying on data centers, a capability it said could become feasible within months as models improve. Tom's Hardware noted the paper shows conceptual capability rather than a demonstrated threat, while The Register dismissed the warning as overstated.
