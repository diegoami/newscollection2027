Anthropic prices IPO at $83, valuation tops $2 trillion in debut

2026-11-17 · 6 outlets · partial

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the expected range, in what outlets called one of history's biggest stock debuts. Shares jumped roughly a third on their Nasdaq debut, pushing Anthropic's valuation above $2 trillion. Reports differ on how much capital was raised, from about $81bn to nearly $105bn, and on the pre-trading valuation of $1.5 trillion fully diluted. CEO Dario Amodei recommitted to pacing AI development while acknowledging real risks.
