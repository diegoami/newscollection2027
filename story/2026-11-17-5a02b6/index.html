Anthropic goes public in one of the biggest IPOs in history

2026-11-17 · 6 outlets · conflicting

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the expected range, and shares jumped more than 30% on their Nasdaq debut, briefly valuing the company at roughly $2 trillion. The deal made its seven founders far wealthier and CEO Dario Amodei both touted AI's promise and warned of its risks. Protesters rallied against the company, and outlets gave differing figures for the funds raised, the valuation, and the first-day share gain.
