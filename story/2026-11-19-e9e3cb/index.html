Amazon campaigns against NYC's Delivery Protection Act with alerts and package labels

2026-11-19 · 3 outlets · full

Amazon is using in-app alerts, homepage banners and shipping labels to warn customers that New York City's proposed Delivery Protection Act could add up to $5 to their orders. The bill is backed by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and labor groups including the Teamsters. Coverage tracks Amazon's campaign expanding from digital notifications to physical stickers on packages.
