Amazon expands campaign against NYC's Delivery Protection Act

2026-11-19 · 2 outlets · full

Amazon is campaigning against New York City's Delivery Protection Act, backed by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and labor groups including the Teamsters, which it says could add up to $5 to orders. The company used homepage banners, in-app pop-ups and push notifications to warn shoppers, and has since begun putting warning labels directly on shipments making the same cost claim.
