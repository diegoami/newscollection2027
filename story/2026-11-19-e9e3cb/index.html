Amazon pushes alerts opposing NYC's Delivery Protection Act

2026-11-19 · 2 outlets · partial

Amazon is showing push notifications, homepage banners and an in-app pop-up warning shoppers that New York City's proposed Delivery Protection Act could add up to $5 per order. The bill is backed by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and labor groups including the Teamsters. One outlet frames this as Amazon using its shopping platform for political purposes.
