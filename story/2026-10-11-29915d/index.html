Ukrainian strikes hit Russian data centers; bot-traffic impact claims later disputed

2026-10-11 · 3 outlets · conflicting

Ukrainian drone strikes knocked out multiple Yandex and other Russian data centers in mid-October, disrupting cloud services, payments, and network connectivity, which fell to about 30% of normal. Early reports said the strikes had nearly eliminated Russian disinformation bot traffic, but later reporting found malicious bots barely affected and bot farms still active, with one outlet acknowledging bot activity was down less than initial reports claimed.
