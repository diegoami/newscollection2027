Amazon's Bee unveils two new AI note-taking and shopping devices

2026-10-15 · 4 outlets · partial

Amazon-owned Bee introduced two new AI devices, Eclipse and Frontier, its first new products since the acquisition, including an updated wearable and a credit-card-sized recorder that attaches to smartphones. The devices can summarize conversations, integrate with Alexa and Amazon shopping, and offer personalized recommendations based on conversations, though one outlet notes a Pro subscription is needed to get the most from them. Outlets give slightly different starting prices, $75 versus $74.99.
