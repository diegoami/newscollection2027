GM to pay $220 million settling driving-data privacy claims

2026-10-29 · 4 outlets · conflicting

GM agreed to pay $220 million to settle claims it illegally sold customers' driving and location data, outlets report. The Verge says the settlement resolves a complaint from 36 state attorneys general, while Engadget puts the number at 35. TechCrunch adds GM must now get drivers' express consent before collecting or sharing such data, and Ars Technica reports the data had been sold to insurance companies.
