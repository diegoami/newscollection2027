GM agrees to pay $220 million to settle data-selling claims

2026-10-29 · 2 outlets · full

General Motors has agreed to pay $220 million to settle claims that it illegally sold drivers' location and driving data to data brokers. The settlement resolves a complaint brought by a coalition of 36 US attorneys general. Going forward, GM must obtain drivers' express consent before collecting or sharing their driving and location data.
