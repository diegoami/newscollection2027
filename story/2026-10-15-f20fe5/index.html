Meta launches Muse Spark Open AI model built to run on Apple hardware

2026-10-15 · 4 outlets · full

Meta released Muse Spark Open, a new open-weight AI model it calls the most capable US open-weight model to date. Built through a partnership with Apple, it runs on Apple silicon using the MLX framework and reportedly needs around 1TB of RAM, running across two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios. Meta also said the model rivals or outperforms comparable models from Chinese developers.
