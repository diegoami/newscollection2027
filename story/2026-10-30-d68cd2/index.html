Wikimedia stakes out middle ground in AI copyright cases

2026-10-30 · 2 outlets · full

The Verge and Engadget report the Wikimedia Foundation is weighing in on landmark AI copyright cases, including the NYT's case against OpenAI. It is pushing courts to protect LLMs training on copyrighted material while not shielding chatbots that regurgitate that work to users, warning that an extreme ruling in either direction could threaten the open web.
