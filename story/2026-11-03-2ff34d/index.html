Apple requires 18+ age ratings for dating apps and AI companions

2026-11-03 · 3 outlets · full

Apple will require 18+ age ratings for dating apps and AI companion apps that offer romantic or sexual content. Under new App Store content descriptors announced Monday, developers must declare whether their apps enable dating with real people or AI companions, and whether they allow users to share their real-world location, so Apple can apply the proper rating.
