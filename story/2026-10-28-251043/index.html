ULA's Vulcan rocket targets return-to-flight launch on Friday the 13th

2026-10-28 · 2 outlets · partial

ULA's Vulcan rocket is set to return to flight on Friday the 13th, in what will be the first launch to use a full complement of six solid rocket boosters. One report frames the mission as critical, citing the risk of SRB nozzle problems, while another describes it neutrally as a return-to-flight launch.
