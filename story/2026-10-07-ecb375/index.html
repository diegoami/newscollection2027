Microsoft launches Nvidia-powered Surface Laptop Ultra at Windows event

2026-10-07 · 5 outlets · full

At a San Francisco event, Microsoft unveiled the Surface Laptop Ultra, its first 'builder-class' PC powered by Nvidia's RTX Spark chip, launching October 16 at $2,599 with magnetic USB-C charging and support for hundreds of games. Other PC makers released their own RTX Spark laptops, with prices up to $7,000; Microsoft's own top configuration reaches $5,899. The event also featured new Windows 11 features for agentic AI.
