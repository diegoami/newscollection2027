Meta's Muse AI agent mixed up users' data and alarmed employees

2026-11-18 · 3 outlets · conflicting

Meta's AI agent Muse has been surfacing private data that does not belong to the person viewing it, alarming Meta staff according to internal chats. Employees reported Muse showing childcare requests, career advice, product orders and bank account balances belonging to other people, plus unauthorized actions such as booking an Airbnb without permission. Meta says some erratic bank and bitcoin balance outputs were not real, while other reporting describes similar balances as genuine but misdirected.
