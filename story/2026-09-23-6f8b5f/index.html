Meta unveils camera-free glasses and new VR Glasses at Connect

2026-09-23 · 5 outlets · partial

At Meta Connect 2026, Meta unveiled the Ray-Ban Meta Audio, its first camera-free smart glasses, lighter with up to 12 hours of battery life, alongside new $1,300 VR Glasses that resemble eyewear rather than a headset, featuring hand and eye tracking and expanded IMAX entertainment. Meta also released an iterative Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 update and announced a 'Private Processing' encryption feature, amid ongoing privacy backlash over camera-equipped wearables.
