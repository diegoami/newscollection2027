Meta unveils camera-free glasses and VR Glasses at Connect

2026-09-23 · 5 outlets · partial

At Meta Connect 2026, Meta introduced its first camera-free smart glasses, Ray-Ban Meta Audio, and new Meta VR Glasses that sit like eyewear rather than a full headset, priced at $1,300 and using hand and eye tracking. Meta also said it is bringing a Private Processing encryption service to its smart glasses. Outlets differ on how impressive or immersive the new VR Glasses are.
