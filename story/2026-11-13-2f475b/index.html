FAA's nationwide ground stop halts US flights for hours

2026-11-13 · 7 outlets · conflicting

The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop that halted US flights for about five hours on Friday, citing a communications failure among air traffic control systems, then lifted it that evening. Transportation Secretary Duffy first blamed routine copper-to-fiber wiring work; Tom's Hardware later reported the FAA traced the glitch to a rogue feature in newly installed routers, and suspended further installs.
