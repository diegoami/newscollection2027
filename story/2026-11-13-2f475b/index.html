FAA ground stop halts US flights for hours amid disputed cause

2026-11-13 · 6 outlets · partial

The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop on Friday, halting US flights for about five hours before lifting it that evening. The government initially cited a communications failure among air traffic control systems, and the transportation secretary later blamed obsolete equipment; he subsequently described the cause as a "routine" equipment upgrade and said there was no evidence anything AI-related was involved.
