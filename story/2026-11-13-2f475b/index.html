FAA's nationwide ground stop halts US flights amid shifting cause reports

2026-11-13 · 7 outlets · conflicting

The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop on November 13, halting US flights for about five hours over what officials initially called a communications failure. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy later attributed it to obsolete equipment and routine copper-to-fiber maintenance work, while a later report said the FAA traced the cause to a malfunctioning feature in two newly installed network routers.
