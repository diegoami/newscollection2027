FCC clears Gulf sovereign funds to take 49.5 percent of Paramount

2026-09-18 · 3 outlets · partial

Three reports say the FCC will let sovereign wealth funds from Gulf states hold 49.5 percent of Paramount-Warner Bros., waiving a rule that caps foreign equity ownership at 25 percent, and that it rejected objections about foreign government influence over the CBS owner. The outlets name the third fund's government differently, one as Abu Dhabi and another as the UAE.
