British Columbia sues OpenAI over Tumbler Ridge school shooting

2026-09-22 · 4 outlets · partial

British Columbia has sued OpenAI and Sam Altman, alleging a mass shooting at a Tumbler Ridge school could have been prevented if OpenAI had warned police that the shooter had used ChatGPT. The suit demands OpenAI pay for a new school, and Tom's Hardware reports OpenAI reviewers reportedly wanted to alert police before the attack.
