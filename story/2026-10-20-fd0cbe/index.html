Google brings Gemini's Personal Intelligence feature to cars via Android Auto

2026-10-20 · 5 outlets · partial

Google is rolling out Gemini's Personal Intelligence feature to cars through Android Auto and vehicles with Google built-in, letting the AI assistant draw on users' Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube and Search data for more personalized responses, such as checking a flight status while driving. Reviewers differ on how comfortable they are with the added access to personal data.
