Google brings Gemini's personal data features to cars, updates ad privacy policy

2026-10-20 · 5 outlets · partial

Google is expanding Gemini's Personal Intelligence feature, which taps Gmail, Calendar, Photos and other services for personalized help, to Android Auto and cars with Google built-in. Alongside this, Google updated its privacy policy to allow Gmail and Photos data to be used for ad targeting when such features are enabled, prompting outlets to differ on whether that is reassuring or concerning.
