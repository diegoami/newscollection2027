Second cyberattack hits European supermarket freezers, spoiling food across countries

2026-11-02 · 6 outlets · partial

A second wave of cyberattacks hit supermarket refrigeration systems across Europe, including in Ireland, Sweden and Belgium, pushing freezer temperatures as high as 50C and spoiling food in a pattern similar to an earlier hack on French shops. UK food suppliers were separately hit by follow-up attacks. Outlets call the disruption 'crippling', while the UK's National Cyber Security Centre says the incidents' national impact has been minimal.
