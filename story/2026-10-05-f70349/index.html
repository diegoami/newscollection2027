OpenAI rolls out invisible text watermarking for ChatGPT and Codex in the EU

2026-10-05 · 3 outlets · full

OpenAI is adding an invisible, machine-readable watermark to ChatGPT and Codex text generated in the European Union, to comply with the EU's AI transparency rules under the AI Act. OpenAI says the approach matches or exceeds methods like Google DeepMind's SynthID, though editing can make the marks harder to detect.
