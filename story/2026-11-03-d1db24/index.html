Google ties school Search access to accepting its AI tools

2026-11-03 · 5 outlets · partial

Google announced new AI products for schools that let them disable some AI tools, such as Gemini, but schools that reject AI altogether lose access to Google Search, since AI is now built into the search engine. Outlets differ on how optional the AI really is, and Wired reports parents and educators worry it hurts children's learning.
