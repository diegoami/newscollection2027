Google says schools that disable its AI tools will lose Search access

2026-11-03 · 5 outlets · partial

Google announced that schools choosing to turn off its AI tools will also lose access to Google Search, because AI features are now built into the search engine. TechCrunch and Engadget describe this as a tradeoff schools must accept, while Wired frames it as Google forcing AI Overviews into classrooms over objections from parents and educators.
