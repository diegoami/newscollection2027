Nine-year-old runs up $118,000 YouTube ad bill on father's company card

2026-09-16 · 2 outlets · partial

Three reports describe a nine-year-old who spent $118,000 on YouTube advertising for his gaming channel using his father's corporate credit card, over roughly three weeks. Outlets differ on which game the channel covered, one naming Minecraft and another Roblox. A later report says the father lost his job over the charges and has declined crowdfunding, while earlier coverage focused on how the money might be repaid.
