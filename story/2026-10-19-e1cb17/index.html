Apple refreshes MacBook Pro and iMac with M6, launches first touchscreen MacBook Pro

2026-10-19 · 8 outlets · partial

Apple updated the 14-inch MacBook Pro and iMac with an M6 chip on October 27th, then unveiled its first touchscreen MacBook Pro with an OLED PROLED XDR display at a 'Pro and tell' event in Los Angeles on October 28th. The touchscreen model starts at $2,899 for 14 inches and $3,399 for 16 inches, with a fully configured version reaching about $14,549.
