Apple unveils first touchscreen MacBook Pro at Los Angeles event

2026-10-19 · 7 outlets · conflicting

Apple held its "Pro and Tell" event in Los Angeles on October 28th and announced its first touchscreen MacBook Pro, with an OLED display it calls PROLED XDR. The laptop starts at $2,899 for 14-inch and $3,399 for 16-inch models, preordering immediately ahead of a November 6th release. Outlets largely agreed on the facts but differed on naming and on how much had changed.
