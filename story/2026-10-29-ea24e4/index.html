Intel's stock crashes despite strong demand for budget chips

2026-10-29 · 4 outlets · partial

On an earnings call, Intel executives said demand for its budget Wildcat Lake laptop chips is outstripping supply and the chips are sold out. Despite that, Intel's broader outlook on its PC business was gloomy, with rising memory and component prices tied to AI demand hurting PC sales. Intel's stock subsequently crashed, with outlets describing the scale of the loss differently, from a quarter of Intel's own value to a $1 trillion one-day hit to the wider semiconductor sector.
