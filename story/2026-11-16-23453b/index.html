iPhone Duo gains limited face unlock in iOS 27.3 beta

2026-11-16 · 5 outlets · partial

Apple is adding a limited face-unlock feature to the iPhone Duo through the iOS 27.3 beta, without new hardware; it is described as recognizing faces but less secure than Face ID. Until now the foldable could only be unlocked with Touch ID or a passcode. Separately, leaked iPhone Duo 2 prototypes suggest future FaceID and Action Button features, some of which may not arrive until a third generation.
