Apple and Signal add new controls to block AI agents from private data

2026-11-16 · 5 outlets · partial

Apple detailed new macOS permission and transparency controls, due in macOS 27.3, to limit AI agents' access to Mac systems. Signal separately rolled out screenshot restrictions on iOS and Android to stop AI assistants such as Siri and Gemini from capturing conversations, extending a protection it first added against Microsoft Recall. One outlet also reported that Apple, Google, and Microsoft were criticized for not giving developers adequate AI agent controls.
