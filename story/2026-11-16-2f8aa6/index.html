Apple and Signal add new protections against AI agents

2026-11-16 · 4 outlets · full

Apple added new macOS security and disk-access controls to limit what AI agents can do on a Mac, part of changes coming in macOS 27.3. Separately, Signal began blocking screenshots by default to stop AI agents from capturing chats, extending a restriction it first added because of Microsoft's Recall feature. The fix was described as incomplete by critics.
