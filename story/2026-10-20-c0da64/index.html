Critical PHP2Shell flaws disclosed, exploited before patching, trigger shutdowns and new warnings

2026-10-20 · 11 outlets · partial

Two critical PHP vulnerabilities known as PHP2Shell forced emergency patches and precautionary website shutdowns across Europe and Canada. Amazon and other researchers found hackers exploiting the flaws roughly four days before patches shipped, and AI tools reportedly helped uncover the bugs. CISA later added PHP2Shell to its exploited-vulnerabilities catalog, warned of a chained Linux kernel flaw, and a separate report found risks to network appliances; a third bug will not get an emergency fix.
