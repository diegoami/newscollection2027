PHP flaw dubbed PHP2Shell forces emergency patches and site shutdowns worldwide

2026-10-20 · 9 outlets · partial

Governments and companies took sites offline as a precaution, and outlets differ on whether OpenAI's AI specifically, or AI generally, helped uncover the flaws.
