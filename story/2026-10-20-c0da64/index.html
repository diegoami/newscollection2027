Critical PHP flaws prompt emergency patches, mass website shutdowns, exploitation

2026-10-20 · 10 outlets · full

PHP's security team warned of two critical flaws, dubbed PHP2Shell, and rushed out emergency patches. Governments and companies across Europe and Canada took sites offline as a precaution; Amazon said attackers had already been exploiting the bugs for days before the patches shipped. OpenAI said its AI model helped find the flaws, affecting roughly 70% of websites, and researchers later found an exploit bypassing the fix, plus a further unpatched RCE flaw.
