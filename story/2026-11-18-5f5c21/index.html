Maryland students sue after AI mistakes Doritos bag for gun

2026-11-18 · 2 outlets · partial

Four current and former students at a Maryland high school are suing after an AI gun-detection alert mistook a bag of Doritos for a weapon, leading to them being handcuffed and detained by armed police. One outlet reports the lawsuit also alleges that over 99% of the school system's AI gun alerts turned out to be false positives.
