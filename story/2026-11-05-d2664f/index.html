OpenSSH 'SSHole' flaw lets attackers bypass Linux passwords

2026-11-05 · 5 outlets · partial

A newly disclosed OpenSSH vulnerability, dubbed "*sshole," lets attackers log into password-disabled Alpine Linux and other musl-based accounts by submitting an oversized password. A patch was issued, but days later outlets reported hackers actively exploiting the flaw to run commands, steal credentials and gain persistent access.
