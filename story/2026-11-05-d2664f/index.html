New OpenSSH and musl flaw lets attackers bypass Linux passwords

2026-11-05 · 4 outlets · full

A newly disclosed vulnerability nicknamed sshole combines flaws in OpenSSH and musl-libc, letting attackers bypass password authentication on Alpine Linux and possibly other musl-based distros by entering an overly long password, over 256 characters per Tom's Hardware. The Register calls the underlying OpenSSH bug decade-old and now fixed.
