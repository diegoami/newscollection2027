Google extends Gemini chat retention, eases AI training opt-out

2026-11-17 · 4 outlets · partial

Google is separating AI-training and chat-history toggles in Gemini's activity settings, making it easier to opt out of having chats used for AI training while still keeping chat history. At the same time, Google is extending how long it retains Gemini chats, from 72 hours to two months, even for users with history off, rolling out globally over coming weeks.
