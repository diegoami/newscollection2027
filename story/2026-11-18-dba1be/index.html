GTA VI launches worldwide; Rockstar reports $3.79 billion in first-day sales

2026-11-18 · 4 outlets · conflicting

Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide after 13 years of development. Rockstar Games later announced the game earned $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day, calling it the biggest entertainment launch in history, after an earlier BBC estimate put first-day sales at just over $2.3 billion. One outlet reported 32.5 million preorder copies sold, and another noted the launch coincided with a Rockstar workers' union vote.
