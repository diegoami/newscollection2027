GTA VI launches worldwide, Rockstar says it made $3.79bn on day one

2026-11-18 · 4 outlets · conflicting

Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide after 13 years in development, with players praising its open world. Rockstar said the game earned $3.79 billion and 32.5 million preorders on its first day, a figure well above an earlier $2.3bn sales estimate. The launch came as Rockstar employees voted on union recognition.
