Ivanti patches Connect Secure zero-day after in-the-wild exploitation, CISA warns

2026-10-13 · 2 outlets · full

Ivanti warned that a zero-day vulnerability in its Connect Secure remote access gateway was being actively exploited, with CISA urging admins to patch immediately given attacks dating to at least October 1st. Ivanti has since released a patch for the flaw and says a fix for a separate privilege-escalation bug is coming in the next few weeks.
