Ukraine used homemade drone relay network to bypass Starlink in bridge strike

2026-11-18 · 2 outlets · partial

Ukrainian forces used a homemade network of relay drones, described as "flying modems," instead of Starlink during a strike on the Crimean Bridge. The setup let them keep a live feed of the attack even as Russia tried to jam the signal, and drew on tactics adapted from battlefield lessons learned from Russia.
