Tesla renames Full Self-Driving to Assisted Driving in Europe

2026-10-09 · 3 outlets · partial

Tesla has renamed its Full Self-Driving feature in Europe, after criticism that the original name misled drivers about the system's capabilities. The rename, meant to help Tesla win regulatory approval in more countries, prompted Germany's transport minister to advocate for Europe-wide adoption. Outlets differ slightly on the exact new name, with one citing 'Tesla Assisted Driving.'
