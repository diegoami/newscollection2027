Adobe brings free Premiere video editing app to Android

2026-09-22 · 3 outlets · full

Adobe has launched its Premiere video-editing app on Android, about a year after it debuted on iOS. The app is free and does not require a Creative Cloud login, though AI features cost extra, and it includes layouts tailored for candy bar phones and foldable devices.
