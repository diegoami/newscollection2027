Nintendo's main Switch cartridge supplier Macronix is exiting ROM chips

2026-11-04 · 4 outlets · full

Macronix, a major supplier of ROM chips for Nintendo Switch cartridges, is phasing out ROM production to focus on NOR and NAND flash memory in demand from AI data centers, according to a Taipei Times report cited by the Verge. The Verge says Macronix's ROM revenue fell 39 percent compared with the prior period, and Engadget and Ars Technica warn the shift could hurt physical Switch games.
