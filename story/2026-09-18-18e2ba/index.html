Researchers used Claude to breach OpenAI employee accounts and code repository

2026-09-18 · 6 outlets · partial

Six outlets report that security researchers used Anthropic's Claude to compromise OpenAI employee accounts and reach the company's internal GitHub repository, then disclosed the flaws rather than exploiting them. Accounts differ on who did it: one names a cybersecurity startup called Hackron AI, another three independent researchers at Hacktron. One report adds that the work took under 72 hours and that a harmless pull request was filed as proof.
