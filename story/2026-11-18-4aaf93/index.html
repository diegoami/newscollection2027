Germ DM encrypted messaging app arrives on Android

2026-11-18 · 2 outlets · partial

Germ DM, an encrypted messaging platform, is now available on Android. It uses what one outlet calls 'burner cards' instead of phone numbers or usernames so users can compartmentalize conversations, and lets people maintain multiple identities without tying them to a phone number. One report describes it as Bluesky's preferred messaging app; the other does not mention that connection.
