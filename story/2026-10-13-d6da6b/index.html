Character.AI files for bankruptcy, selling chatbot assets toward a Disney deal

2026-10-13 · 3 outlets · partial

Character.AI, one of the first major generative-AI chatbot startups, filed for bankruptcy after facing mounting scrutiny and lawsuits over its chatbots' safety. The company is selling some assets to Disney for $20 million, though outlets differ on how final that deal is: some describe it as agreed, while Ars Technica frames Disney's $20M as still just an offer.
