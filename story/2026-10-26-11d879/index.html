Anthropic files for US IPO, disclosing finances, risks and governance structure

2026-10-26 · 9 outlets · partial

Anthropic filed an S-1 prospectus with the SEC to go public, disclosing revenue growth, mounting losses, and a governance structure meant to preserve founder control. The filing also warns its AI models could pose safety risks, including resisting shutdown. Outlets report differing valuation estimates, debate whether the lengthy filing omits financial details, and note Amazon's large ownership stake and the AI industry's growing reliance on natural gas for power.
