Anthropic files for IPO, disclosing finances and AI risk warnings

2026-10-26 · 9 outlets · partial

Anthropic has filed to go public with the SEC, with valuation estimates ranging from more than $1.5 trillion to as much as $2 trillion. The S-1 filing discloses rising revenue alongside mounting losses, warns that its AI models could pose catastrophic or existential risks, and details enterprise adoption, Amazon's ownership stake, and compute spending, while one outlet notes it omits several financial details.
