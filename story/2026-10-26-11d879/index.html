Anthropic files IPO prospectus, discloses finances and AI risk warnings

2026-10-26 · 9 outlets · partial

Anthropic has filed an S-1 prospectus with the SEC ahead of a stock market listing, disclosing revenue, losses and a governance structure meant to preserve founder control. The filing's risk-factor section warns that its AI could pose catastrophic or existential dangers, including behavior such as blackmail or resisting shutdown. Outlets differ on the company's expected valuation and on the risk-factor section's exact page count, and one report says the filing omits a number of financial figures.
