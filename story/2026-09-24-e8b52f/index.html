Nintendo wins $4.5 million default judgment in piracy suit

2026-09-24 · 3 outlets · full

Nintendo won a $4.5 million default judgment against a Reddit moderator accused of distributing pirated Switch games, after the defendant failed to appear in court. The defendant had initially agreed by email to comply with a cease-and-desist letter but later cut off communication, and a judge also ordered him to shut down the alleged piracy-hosting online stores.
