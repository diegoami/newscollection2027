Microsoft offers buyouts to 60,000 workers, then posts record quarterly revenue

2026-10-26 · 8 outlets · conflicting

Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees, about a quarter of its workforce, citing cost-cutting and rising AI spending. Days later it reported record fiscal Q1 2027 revenue of $92.3 billion, up 19%, with Azure revenue disclosed for the first time at $33.2 billion and $10 billion in revenue tied to OpenAI. Outlets differ on the exact profit figure, and on whether to describe the buyouts as voluntary or as layoffs.
