Antigravity adds wristwatch control bundle for its A1 drone

2026-10-20 · 2 outlets · full

Antigravity has launched a new Follow Me bundle for its A1 drone that replaces its VR headset with a watch and ring remote for gesture-based tracking and control. The bundle is available as an accessory or as a full kit with the drone, and one review says it effectively halves the drone's price.
