Tesla plans to start selling Cybercab robotaxis by year end

2026-10-22 · 2 outlets · partial

Tesla says it will begin selling its driverless, steering-wheel-less Cybercab before the end of the year and expand the rollout to more US cities, even as federal regulators review the vehicle's safety and Tesla withholds its paid-ride numbers. Reports differ on whether the buyers are individual customers or other transport companies.
