Citizen Lab exposes zero-click spyware delivered via targeted online ads

2026-10-15 · 7 outlets · partial

Citizen Lab researchers at the University of Toronto disclosed the first technical evidence of mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online advertising, compromising devices at Al Jazeera Media Network without any user interaction. An Israeli adtech company was implicated in enabling the delivery. Outlets differed on what to call the spyware or software involved, variously naming it AdFection, Candiru spyware, and Discover-Tech's Controlium.
