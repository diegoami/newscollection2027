US suspends Microsoft, Adobe and other firms from worker green card program

2026-10-08 · 9 outlets · conflicting

The Trump administration suspended Microsoft, Adobe and several other companies, including major IT outsourcing firms, from a program that lets employers sponsor foreign workers for US permanent residency. Vice President JD Vance announced the move Thursday, accusing Microsoft of abusing the visa system more than any other company; Microsoft disputes the claim. Outlets differ on the total number of firms affected, citing seven or eight companies.
