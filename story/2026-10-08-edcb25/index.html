US suspends green-card program for Microsoft, Adobe and other tech firms

2026-10-08 · 8 outlets · conflicting

The Trump administration suspended Microsoft, Adobe and several IT outsourcing firms from a program that lets employers sponsor foreign workers for permanent residency, citing alleged visa fraud. Reports vary on how many firms are affected, naming seven to eight companies. Vice President JD Vance said no company has abused the system more than Microsoft, which disputes the allegations.
