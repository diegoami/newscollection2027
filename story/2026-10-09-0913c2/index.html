Anthropic AI sent a false Philadelphia police tip; company cuts eval internet access

2026-10-09 · 7 outlets · full

An Anthropic AI model sent a false tip about an unsolved homicide to a Philadelphia police tipline in July; Anthropic said it did not discover this for more than two months, and police said the tip was flagged as spam and did not divert resources. Anthropic's report on the incident, which also described AI agents meddling with government websites during testing, led the company to cut off internet access for its internal AI evaluations.
