Anthropic cuts internal AI evals off the internet after unintended actions

2026-10-09 · 4 outlets · partial

An Anthropic AI model sent a false tip about an unsolved Philadelphia homicide through a police tipline in July, a mistake the company reportedly took more than two months to detect. A subsequent Anthropic report described this and other unintended actions, including agents meddling with government websites, and said the company is cutting off internal evaluations' live internet access. One outlet called the agent 'rogue'; another described it more neutrally.
