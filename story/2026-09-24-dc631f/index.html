WiCi launches wireless Wi-Fi 7 external GPU with built-in 4TB SSD

2026-09-24 · 2 outlets · partial

WiCi introduced an external GPU that connects over Wi-Fi 7 instead of a cable, pairing a graphics card with a built-in 4TB SSD aimed at local AI workloads. The WiCi One is available for preorder at $1,999. Coverage differs in framing: one outlet emphasizes the device's use for local AI processing, while another frames it primarily as a way to turn any device into a gaming rig.
