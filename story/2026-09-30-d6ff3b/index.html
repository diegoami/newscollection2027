Xbox's Disc to Digital feature rolls out to all players

2026-09-30 · 2 outlets · full

Microsoft's Xbox Disc to Digital feature has rolled out to all players, supporting most Xbox Series X and Xbox One games. It gives a digital license tied to a user's physical disc, letting them play without inserting it, though selling the disc revokes access.
