Bitget crypto hack blamed on North Korea, loss estimate rises

2026-09-25 · 4 outlets · partial

Bitget disclosed that suspected North Korean hackers stole roughly $351 million from its wallets, a figure later revised to $387.5 million as Bitget formally blamed North Korea, citing IP addresses tied to VPN infrastructure the group has used before. Bitget has since resumed Bitcoin withdrawals that were suspended after the breach.
