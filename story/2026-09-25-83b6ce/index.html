Bitget hack blamed on North Korea, loss total shifts over time

2026-09-25 · 4 outlets · conflicting

Bitget disclosed a large crypto theft it attributes to suspected North Korean hackers, citing VPN infrastructure tied to the group. The exchange later resumed withdrawals and said attackers exploited a zero-day flaw in third-party security products. Reported loss totals vary across outlets and over time, from about $351 million to $387.5 million.
