UK police watchdog warns facial recognition risks wrongful arrests

2026-11-12 · 2 outlets · full

A UK police watchdog report found that facial recognition technology risks causing wrongful arrests, particularly of ethnic minorities, after inspectors found some officers acted on retrospective-search matches without sufficient checks. The report says the technology can aid crime-fighting but is undermined by errors, bias and inconsistent governance, and calls for change.
