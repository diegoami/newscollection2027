OpenAI drops NDAs, pledges to protect data center communities

2026-11-05 · 2 outlets · partial

OpenAI announced new commitments to communities near its data center projects, including protecting local resources, reporting impact, and no longer using non-disclosure agreements with public officials. The Verge ties the move to this week's midterms, where voters opposed data center expansion, while Engadget notes OpenAI says the pledges predate that vote.
