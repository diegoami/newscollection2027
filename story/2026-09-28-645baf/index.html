Google to end ChromeOS support in 2034, shift to Googlebook OS

2026-09-28 · 4 outlets · partial

Google confirmed ChromeOS support will end in 2034 as the company transitions to a new Googlebook operating system, with some recent Chromebooks able to switch over. The Register frames this as ending support two years early, after eight years rather than the expected decade, while Ars Technica describes the plan as only seemingly confirmed.
