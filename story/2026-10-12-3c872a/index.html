CMF unveils its first portable Bluetooth speakers, shaped like milk cartons

2026-10-12 · 2 outlets · full

CMF, the budget-focused brand that Nothing created and is spinning off into an India-based subsidiary, announced its first portable Bluetooth speakers, the CMF Speaker Series. The milk-carton-shaped speakers can be paired together and work with Auracast, continuing the affordable, distinctively designed approach of earlier CMF products like the Headphone Pro.
