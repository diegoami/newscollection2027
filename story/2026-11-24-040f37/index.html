Huawei convicted of stealing US trade secrets, acquitted of racketeering

2026-11-24 · 7 outlets · full

A US jury convicted Huawei on 10 of 12 counts, including stealing trade secrets, money laundering, fraud and evading Iran sanctions, while acquitting it of racketeering. The charges, dating back to 2019, followed a months-long New York trial. Attorney General Todd Blanche said no person or company is above the law, and prosecutors said Huawei used front company names and did little to hide the thefts.
