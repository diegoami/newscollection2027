Apple launches HomeView hub, Home Collection devices, and refreshed Apple TV and HomePod mini

2026-10-06 · 7 outlets · partial

At its October 13 "Welcome Home" event in New York, Apple introduced the HomeView smart display for $299, a Home Collection of cameras, thermostats, a smart lock, and a video doorbell made with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric, a refreshed $299 Apple TV 4K with far-field microphones and Siri AI arriving November 13, and a $149 HomePod mini 2 with a new chip arriving October 30, whose Siri AI features come later.
