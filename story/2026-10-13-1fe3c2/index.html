OpenAI engineer arrested for allegedly planting hidden recording device at office

2026-10-13 · 9 outlets · partial

OpenAI said it found a hidden recording device, concealed in toilet paper, in a meeting room used by top executives, and referred the matter to the FBI. A Saudi national engineer, identified as Abdulrahman Alfozan, was arrested and charged with intercepting oral communications, facing up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine. The FBI said the device aimed to capture sensitive superintelligence-related communications.
