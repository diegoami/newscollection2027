Tidal to ban fully AI-generated music, delete millions of tracks

2026-11-24 · 5 outlets · conflicting

Tidal announced it will ban fully AI-generated music starting December 9th while still allowing AI-assisted songs, deleting tens of millions of tracks. Outlets gave different deletion counts, roughly 29 million versus 27 million. Tidal said AI-generated tracks make up a large share of recent uploads and that such music diverts royalties from artists.
