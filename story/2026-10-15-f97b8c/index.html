Tesla's new Roadster demo divides outlets over SpaceX rocket vs stunt-gas tech

2026-10-15 · 6 outlets · conflicting

Tesla unveiled its next-generation Roadster at a Texas demo event where it drove upside down, flew briefly, and leapt over other Tesla models using thrusters, with reservations costing $250,000. Some coverage described the propulsion as SpaceX rocket thruster technology making it the world's first flying car, while other outlets said the thrusters were 50-year-old cold-gas stunt technology comparable to older missile engineering, and argued the flying feat was more showmanship than a genuine production capability.
