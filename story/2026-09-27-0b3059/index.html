CISA orders Citrix NetScaler patch after zero-days exploited

2026-09-27 · 2 outlets · partial

Citrix confirmed two critical NetScaler remote code execution zero-days, tracked as CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, are being exploited in attacks and released fixes. CISA then ordered U.S. federal agencies to patch the exploited flaws by Wednesday, in an update one outlet says also fixed a third critical vulnerability and five other serious issues.
