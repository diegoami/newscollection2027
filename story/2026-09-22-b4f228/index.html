Discord Rolls Out Automatic Age Estimation After Backlash

2026-09-22 · 5 outlets · full

Discord rolled out a revised, largely automatic age-verification system after backlash over earlier measures, according to Engadget, TechCrunch, The Verge, The Register and Ars Technica. TechCrunch says Discord expects 90% of users won't need to verify, and The Verge says the rollout excludes Australia and the UK.
