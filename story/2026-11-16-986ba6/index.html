Palantir's NHS England contract to run until 2029 before ending

2026-11-16 · 3 outlets · partial

UK ministers decided Palantir's NHS England data contract will continue until 2029, to give time to line up British replacement suppliers, despite calls from some Labour MPs to end the arrangement sooner. Critics said the extension gives the US supplier more time to entrench itself, while the government framed the move as ending, rather than renewing, the contract.
