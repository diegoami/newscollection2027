Anthropic pitches AI safety bill requiring judge approval to block a model

2026-10-18 · 2 outlets · partial

Anthropic has proposed federal AI safety legislation that would let authorities act only when a model poses a risk of 50 or more deaths or $1 billion in damages. Evaluators would have to report imminent risk to the Department of Commerce within 72 hours, but the department could not block a model's release without first presenting evidence to a federal judge and obtaining a court order.
