Hackers breach ccTLD registries to mint fake certificates for Google

2026-10-06 · 3 outlets · full

Attackers compromised domain registries and used the breach to hijack top-level domains and obtain unauthorized HTTPS certificates impersonating Google and other large services. The fake certificates allowed trusted-brand impersonation without triggering normal browser warnings; affected ccTLDs include Ghana, American Samoa, and Sierra Leone.
