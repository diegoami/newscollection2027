AMD to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion

2026-09-28 · 5 outlets · full

AMD agreed to acquire World Labs, the AI company co-founded by Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal reported at about $8.2 billion. Li will join AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist. World Labs launched in 2024 and had reached a $1 billion valuation; the deal is expected to close by year's end.
