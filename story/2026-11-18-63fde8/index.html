Voyager 1 passes one light-day from Earth, then pauses data transmission

2026-11-18 · 4 outlets · partial

Voyager 1, launched 49 years ago, has reached or is about to reach a distance of one light-day from Earth, with a signal now taking about 24 hours to travel each way. Outlets differ on whether the milestone has already been achieved or is still anticipated. The probe stores data on an eight-track tape recorder. A later report says Voyager 1's carrier signal still reaches Earth but it has stopped returning science and engineering data.
