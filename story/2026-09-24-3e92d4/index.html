Meta unveils Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi-like AI wearable

2026-09-23 · 4 outlets · partial

Meta unveiled Muse Charm, a small Tamagotchi-like hardware device housing its Muse AI agent, resembling a strapless smartwatch with a lanyard. Muse is also coming to Meta's smart glasses. Muse Charm devices will reportedly be able to recognize and interact with each other once they launch later this year.
