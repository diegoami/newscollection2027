Meta unveils Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi-like AI wearable

2026-09-23 · 4 outlets · partial

Meta has unveiled the Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi-like wearable built around its Muse AI agent, resembling a chunky smartwatch without a strap. The devices will be able to recognize and interact with each other when they launch later this year, and Muse is also coming to Meta's AI glasses. The Register jokingly compares the device's care needs to a real Tamagotchi.
