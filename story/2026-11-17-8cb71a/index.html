Flipper Devices crowdfunds $399 Flipper One Linux multi-tool

2026-11-17 · 2 outlets · full

Flipper Devices has launched a crowdfunding campaign for the Flipper One, a $399 portable Linux computer and networking multi-tool positioned as a successor to the Flipper Zero. The campaign has raised $13M on Kickstarter, with backers favoring a $759 Ultimate Bundle, though Flipper has warned that rising RAM prices could affect production.
