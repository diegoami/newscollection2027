Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigns over affairs, then is reinstated as CEO

2026-11-06 · 10 outlets · partial

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was investigated, then resigned, after reports of an affair with a Chinese-intelligence-linked executive and a separate affair with an Nvidia subordinate. The scandal drew a congressional summons, shareholder lawsuits, a stock slide and a reported FBI inquiry. CFO Colette Kress served as interim CEO for about eleven days. A reinstatement campaign backed by Elon Musk and other tech leaders, plus pressure from President Trump, preceded the board's unanimous vote to bring Huang back.
