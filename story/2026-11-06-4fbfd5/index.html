Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns after Chinese spy affair scandal and congressional probe

2026-11-06 · 9 outlets · partial

Nvidia opened an investigation after reports that CEO Jensen Huang had an affair with a Chinese businesswoman linked to Chinese intelligence, which drew congressional scrutiny, shareholder lawsuits and a stock decline. A second affair allegation involving an Nvidia subordinate followed, and the board moved to remove Huang. He resigned as CEO, president and board member, and an interim CFO replacement was named, with a third affair reported afterward and a congressional hearing agreed.
