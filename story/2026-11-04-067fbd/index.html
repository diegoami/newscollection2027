Hackers raise temperatures at French blood storage facilities in cyberattack

2026-11-04 · 4 outlets · partial

France's national blood service said hackers accessed control systems and raised temperatures at three cold stores holding blood products, part of a wider wave of attacks on European refrigeration systems. The Register reports attackers pushed cold-room controls as high as 110°C but says safeguards prevented stock losses, while BleepingComputer attributes the breach to a compromised refrigeration equipment provider.
