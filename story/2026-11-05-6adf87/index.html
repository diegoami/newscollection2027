FAA moves to retire floppy disks from air traffic control

2026-11-05 · 4 outlets · full

The FAA, under Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, announced a plan to replace the 3.5-inch floppy disks still used in parts of air traffic control with a digital system, aiming for display systems to be floppy-free at 435 sites by the end of 2028, though more funding is needed to finish the overhaul.
