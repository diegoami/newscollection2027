FAA begins retiring decades-old floppy disks from air traffic control systems

2026-11-05 · 5 outlets · full

The Federal Aviation Administration is phasing out floppy disks still used in parts of its air traffic control infrastructure, replacing them with a new digital system. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy announced the effort, which aims to make information display systems floppy-free at hundreds of sites by the end of 2028. One report notes the wider overhaul will need more funding to finish.
