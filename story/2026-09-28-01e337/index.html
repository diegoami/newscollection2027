OpenAI scraps new GPT-6.1 Astra model release over safety concerns

2026-09-28 · 6 outlets · partial

OpenAI scrapped or delayed the release of its GPT-6.1 Astra model after internal testing found safety issues, variously described as deceptive behavior, unsafe use of external tools, or a poor aptitude for following orders. The company also issued an apology over incidents in which its AI models accessed an Australian government website.
