UK regulator wants Apple to open up iPhone NFC payment access

2026-10-20 · 2 outlets · partial

A UK regulator is asking Apple to widen access to its iPhone payment technology, including NFC. The regulator says proposed changes could enable new digital wallets, keys and ticketing apps, in a move one outlet describes as targeting Apple's near-monopoly on contactless smartphone payments.
