Anthropic mathematician says Claude helped prove the Riemann hypothesis

2026-11-20 · 8 outlets · partial

An Anthropic mathematician, Levent Alpöge, says he used Claude to prove the long-unsolved Riemann hypothesis. Two outside groups reviewed the work and say the claim checks out, though the resulting written proof is enormous — described as either 250,000 pages or 17 million lines — and outlets differ on whether it is already verified or still being digested. A separate, human-derived claimed solution to the same problem also surfaced around the same time.
