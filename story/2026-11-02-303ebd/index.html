Google dismantles 1.3-million-device Android TV botnet, bans maker

2026-11-02 · 3 outlets · partial

A botnet built from roughly 1.3 million infected Android TV and set-top boxes has been dismantled; outlets say the manufacturer, Videostrong, intentionally shipped devices with a hidden backdoor and has now been banned from building certified Android devices. One outlet attributes the takedown to law enforcement, while others credit Google and its partners.
