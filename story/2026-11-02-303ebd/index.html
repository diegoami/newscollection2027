Google dismantles botnet built into 1.3 million Android TV boxes

2026-11-02 · 5 outlets · full

Google and partners disrupted a botnet running on more than 1.3 million Android TV set-top boxes after discovering that manufacturer Videostrong Technology Co. had secretly built a backdoor into the devices. Google says it dismantled the backdoor, cut ties with Videostrong, and banned the China-based company from building or selling certified Android devices.
