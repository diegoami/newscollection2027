Google dismantles 1.3 million-device Android TV botnet, bans manufacturer

2026-11-02 · 4 outlets · partial

Google and industry partners dismantled a botnet built into more than 1.3 million Android TV boxes after discovering that manufacturing partner Videostrong Technology Co. had secretly built in a backdoor used to generate cybercrime revenue. Google has banned Videostrong from building or selling certified Android devices. One outlet credits the takedown to law enforcement, calling it a 'cops bust', while others attribute the action to Google itself.
