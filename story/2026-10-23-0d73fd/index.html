Apple confirms cracked iPhone Duo screens, offers replacements as teardowns probe cause

2026-10-23 · 9 outlets · partial

Reports surfaced of iPhone Duo units cracking at the hinge soon after launch. Apple confirmed a 'small number' of devices are affected and offered AppleCare replacements. Independent teardowns by iFixit and a YouTuber pointed to the folding hinge and crease design as a likely cause, while one outlet questioned whether the problem is more widespread than Apple's characterization suggests.
