iPhone Duo screens crack as hinge issues emerge; Apple acknowledges and offers repairs

2026-10-23 · 7 outlets · partial

Early iPhone Duo units are developing cracked inner screens, with reports pointing to the hinge and folding display. Teardowns by iFixit and an independent investigator point to hinge- and dust-related causes, while Apple has acknowledged an issue affecting a small number of units and is offering AppleCare replacements for those affected.
