Hack exposes data on tens of thousands of Homeland Security employees, including undercover agents

2026-10-28 · 6 outlets · partial

A hack exposed personal data on more than 41,000 Department of Homeland Security employees, including undercover ICE and CBP agents, according to multiple outlets. The leaked records reportedly include phone numbers, office locations, and group memberships tied to surveillance programs. Outlets differ on who is responsible: some describe a lone hacker, while others attribute the breach to a named hacktivist group, the Department of Peace.
