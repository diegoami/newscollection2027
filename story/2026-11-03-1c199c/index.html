Apple Watch's Audio Intelligence listening features enter watchOS 27.3 beta

2026-11-03 · 4 outlets · full

Apple's new Audio Intelligence features for Apple Watch, Siri Recap and Live Rewind, have entered beta testing through the watchOS 27.3 developer beta, available on the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4. First revealed at Apple's September event, the opt-in features use ambient listening to summarize conversations and generate transcripts of recent audio, prompting one outlet to call them controversial.
