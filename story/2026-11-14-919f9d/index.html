Italy fines Meta over Facebook photo access without consent

2026-11-14 · 3 outlets · full

Italy's data protection authority fined Meta roughly €805m ($887m) after finding its Facebook app accessed users' photos and videos without specific consent. One report says 20 million users were not informed. Meta reportedly plans to appeal the decision.
