DoorDash agrees to acquire Instacart for about $17 billion

2026-10-29 · 3 outlets · full

DoorDash announced it is acquiring grocery-delivery rival Instacart in a deal valued at roughly $17 billion. The acquisition pairs DoorDash's restaurant-delivery business, which already includes its own grocery service, with Instacart's grocery marketplace and retailer partnerships. Outlets describe the deal as uniting two of the largest players in on-demand and grocery delivery in the US.
