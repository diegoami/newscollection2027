Apple's $250 Million Siri Settlement Opens for iPhone Owner Claims

2026-09-21 · 4 outlets · full

Apple is paying $250 million to settle claims it failed to deliver an AI-upgraded Siri, and eligible iPhone 15 Pro, Pro Max and iPhone 16 owners can now file a claim worth up to $95 each, according to The Verge, ZDNet, Wired and Engadget. Wired says the deadline to submit a claim is December 21.
