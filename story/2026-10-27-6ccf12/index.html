Apple brings M6 chip to 14-inch MacBook Pro and iMac, skips 16-inch model

2026-10-27 · 6 outlets · full

Apple updated the 14-inch MacBook Pro and the iMac with its new M6 chip, while the 16-inch MacBook Pro keeps the older M5. The refreshed iMac gains more cores and better per-core performance, and the MacBook Pro adds Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 support and a 24-hour battery rating. The M6 is described as bringing more cores, faster memory, a larger NPU and wider connectivity across more Apple products.
