Tesla winds down Cybertruck production to make way for Cybercab robotaxi

2026-11-06 · 7 outlets · partial

Tesla is winding down Cybertruck production, with Elon Musk saying the freed factory capacity will go toward the Cybercab robotaxi. Musk defended the decision and said some customer deposits would be refunded, while the move drew backlash from some Cybertruck owners. Commentary was split: some outlets called the truck a commercial failure, while others framed its end as a boost for the broader EV market and battery supply chain.
