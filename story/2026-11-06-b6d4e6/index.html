Tesla winds down Cybertruck production to make room for Cybercab robotaxis

2026-11-06 · 7 outlets · partial

Tesla is winding down Cybertruck production, with Elon Musk saying the Texas factory will be repurposed to build the Cybercab robotaxi within about eight months. Musk later defended the decision and said some deposits would be refunded. The move drew backlash from Cybertruck owners, with outlets split on whether the end marks a failure or benefits the broader EV market.
