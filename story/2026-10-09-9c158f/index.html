NASA releases trove of Artemis II photos, video and audio data to the public

2026-10-09 · 2 outlets · conflicting

NASA this week made a large trove of Artemis II mission data publicly available, including photographs, video, audio and astronauts' notes intended to support study of the Moon and future missions. The data was posted on NASA's 'Artemis II: Sights & Sounds of Science' site and includes more than 11,000 photos and videos and over 8.5 hours of audio. Outlets differ on the total size, citing nearly 1 TB and over 800GB respectively.
