Meta edits terms after backlash over AI training on scraped news content

2026-10-16 · 3 outlets · partial

Meta's new commercial terms initially allowed it to crawl and use content from affiliated business sites, including news publishers, to train its AI. After backlash, Meta removed that language, though a spokesperson said the edit does not reflect any change in how Meta actually crawls the web. The revised terms still do not commit to respecting robots.txt.
