Meta removes news-scraping language from its terms after backlash

2026-10-16 · 2 outlets · partial

Meta's new commercial terms, set to take effect October 30th, initially appeared to permit it to scrape content from websites, including news publishers, to train its AI. After backlash, Meta updated its terms again to remove that language. A Meta spokesperson said the revision does not reflect any actual change in how the company crawls the web, even as coverage describes the move as walking back a scraping permission.
