Meta delays new business terms after backlash over AI content scraping

2026-10-16 · 4 outlets · partial

Meta revised and then delayed enforcement of new business terms, after backlash over language that could let it scrape content from sites linked to businesses on its platforms, including news publishers, to train AI. A spokesperson said the revisions don't change how Meta crawls the web; legal experts say the changes still fall short.
