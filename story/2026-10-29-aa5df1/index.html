Ofcom sets out age-check options for UK's under-16 social media ban

2026-10-29 · 2 outlets · partial

UK media regulator Ofcom has outlined accepted methods for verifying users' ages ahead of a ban on social media access for under-16s, including photo ID, banking checks and facial age estimation. One report notes some adults using affected platforms may also need to verify their age, though not all adults will be required to.
