Google's CC AI agent takes on household and group coordination

2026-09-17 · 3 outlets · partial

Three reports describe Google's CC, an AI agent for families and groups that shares members' emails, schedules and tasks so it can manage calendars, fill in forms, make shopping lists and plan meals. One outlet says the agent gets its own Google account and sends a daily briefing. The texts differ on whether CC is a new experimental product or a relaunch of an existing agent.
