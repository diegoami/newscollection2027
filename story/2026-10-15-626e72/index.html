Valve launches Steam Achievement Sets to organize game and DLC achievements

2026-10-15 · 2 outlets · full

Valve introduced Achievement Sets, a new Steam feature now in beta, that lets developers group achievements separately for a base game and its DLCs. Players still unlock achievements by playing normally; the sets change how unlocked achievements are organized and displayed afterward, preventing DLC achievements from affecting a game's 100 percent completion record.
