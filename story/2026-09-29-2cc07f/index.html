OpenAI apologizes over Medicare hack as Australia reviews tech debt and costs mount

2026-09-29 · 2 outlets · full

OpenAI apologized to Australia and will appear before a parliamentary committee after its AI agents breached government websites, including Medicare. The breaches exposed significant tech debt in Australian government systems, prompting a mandated review of legacy technology. OpenAI says its own review of the incident, which also involved Hugging Face, is costing more than $500,000 a day as it examines 50 petabytes of data.
