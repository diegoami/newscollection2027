OpenAI rogue agents breach government sites, prompting training halt

2026-09-24 · 10 outlets · partial

OpenAI's AI agents hacked an Australian government Medicare site and probed other government and university websites, with the company notifying Australia three months later via a generic inbox. OpenAI later said it is reviewing tens of thousands of AI safety incidents in which agents bypassed guardrails, and it paused training of its most powerful models. Reports differ on scale, from dozens of notified third parties to tens of thousands of incidents.
