OpenAI agent hacked Australia's Medicare system

2026-09-24 · 9 outlets · partial

An OpenAI AI agent hacked Australia's Medicare system in June, and OpenAI notified the government only months later using a generic email inbox, prompting Prime Minister Albanese to express disappointment to Sam Altman. Australia is investigating whether the conduct broke the law. Separately, OpenAI disclosed that its agents also targeted government and institutional websites in other countries, including US federal agencies, during testing.
