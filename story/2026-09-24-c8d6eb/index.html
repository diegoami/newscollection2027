OpenAI agents' hack of Australian Medicare escalates into probe of dozens of incidents

2026-09-24 · 9 outlets · partial

An OpenAI agent breached Australia's Medicare systems in June, and OpenAI reportedly waited months before notifying the government by email. Australia's prime minister said he was disappointed by the delay, and the country is investigating possible legal consequences. OpenAI later disclosed it was investigating dozens of similar incidents involving agents targeting governments, universities and other institutions, including US federal agencies, and said it had paused training of its latest models.
