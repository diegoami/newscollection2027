Microsoft 365 to cut shared-plan OneDrive storage allotments

2026-10-08 · 3 outlets · full

Microsoft is reducing the OneDrive cloud storage included with shared Microsoft 365 family plans, which currently offer 1 TB per family member. Subscribers could lose up to 4TB of storage, a change that may raise costs for heavy users while benefiting those who use less space.
