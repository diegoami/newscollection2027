Lenders worry SpaceX's orbital chip collateral would be unreachable if Musk defaults

2026-11-20 · 3 outlets · full

In a $43 billion Nvidia GPU financing deal for SpaceX, a separate company buys the chips while SpaceX pays quarterly to use them. Lenders are reportedly uneasy that if the chips are launched into orbit for space data centers, the collateral could become impossible to reclaim should SpaceX default, so bondholders want the collateral kept on Earth and within reach.
