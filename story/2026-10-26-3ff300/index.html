OpenAI announces new discoveries in math, computer science and physics

2026-10-26 · 4 outlets · partial

OpenAI announced three new findings in mathematics, computer science and physics, including a model-generated proof that the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false, using external expert "shepherds" to vet the results. The announcement follows OpenAI's release of math manuscripts weeks earlier. Outlets differ on credit for the proof: some attribute it to OpenAI alone, others to multiple frontier labs including Anthropic, and some researchers say they are tiring of OpenAI's approach to sharing breakthroughs.
