State AGs challenge Character.AI's plan to sell user chats

2026-11-25 · 3 outlets · full

A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is trying to block Character.AI from selling users' private chat histories as part of its Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. Officials say the proposed sale covers data from more than 21 million U.S. accounts, including 2.1 million minors, with some conversations described as explicit.
