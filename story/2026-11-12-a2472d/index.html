Microsoft 365 outage disrupts Outlook, Teams, Copilot worldwide before full recovery

2026-11-12 · 11 outlets · conflicting

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot for about a day, Microsoft initially blaming "elevated load" and later a technical failure or bad update, not a cyberattack. Reports disagreed on whether a cyberattack had been ruled out and on how long the outage lasted, with estimates ranging from a day-long disruption to nearly two days. Governments, hospitals, schools and businesses were affected. Microsoft later said service was fully restored.
