Microsoft 365 outage disrupts Outlook, Teams worldwide for a day

2026-11-12 · 11 outlets · conflicting

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage disrupted Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide, affecting governments, hospitals and schools. Microsoft initially cited elevated load, then said servers unexpectedly became unavailable, and finally blamed a bad update rather than a cyberattack. Reports differ on whether a cyberattack was ruled out early on and on the outage's total duration.
