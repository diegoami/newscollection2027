Microsoft 365 global outage, day-long disruption, and its root-cause bug

2026-11-12 · 11 outlets · conflicting

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide, disrupting governments, hospitals and courts for roughly a day. Microsoft said a bad update, not a cyberattack, was responsible, and later attributed it to a software bug introduced in a July fix that triggered a cascading failure. A hotel's Outlook-dependent safety system failed during the outage, leading to a chlorine gas incident that hospitalized guests. Services were later fully restored.
