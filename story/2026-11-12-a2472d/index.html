Microsoft 365 outage takes down Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide

2026-11-12 · 11 outlets · conflicting

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot for roughly a day, affecting governments, hospitals, schools and businesses worldwide, with some Japanese workers resorting to fax machines. Microsoft gave shifting explanations for the cause, and outlets disagree on whether a cyberattack was ruled out and on exactly how long the disruption lasted.
