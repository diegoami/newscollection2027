Jensen Huang apologizes to Nvidia staff at first all-hands since return

2026-11-24 · 5 outlets · partial

At his first all-hands meeting since being reinstated as Nvidia CEO, Jensen Huang apologized to staff, acknowledged violating the company's code of conduct over extramarital affairs, and discussed therapy and his 'tech bro' reputation. He denied receiving special government treatment. Outlets differ on whether he distanced himself from White House involvement in his return or thanked the White House during the meeting.
