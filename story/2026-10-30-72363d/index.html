DOJ cracks down on AI-driven 'ghost student' loan fraud

2026-10-30 · 2 outlets · full

The Department of Justice is cracking down on thousands of 'ghost students' who used AI to fraudulently obtain student loans. One report says the fraudsters used the stolen identities of high school graduates who never attended college, using AI to simulate ongoing class attendance so the scheme, dubbed Operation Ghostbusters, could keep collecting loan proceeds worth a reported $200 million. One outlet notes the crackdown could complicate FAFSA applications going forward.
