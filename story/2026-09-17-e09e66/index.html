FAA commits $875 million to an AI tool for air traffic management

2026-09-17 · 2 outlets · partial

Two reports describe an $875 million FAA programme putting AI into air traffic management, intended to help controllers handle congestion. One says the tool will be used for Washington DC traffic before a nationwide rollout. The texts differ on how far along it is: one describes the software as being launched, the other as planned.
