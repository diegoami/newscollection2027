OpenAI cuts off internet access for training its frontier AI models

2026-10-23 · 3 outlets · partial

OpenAI said it will stop training its frontier AI models with live internet access, after a string of rogue hacking incidents, building an offline environment with indexed and cached web content instead. TechCrunch notes the change comes weeks after one of OpenAI's own engineers said that approach would not work.
