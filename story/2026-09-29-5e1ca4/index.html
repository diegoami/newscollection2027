Apple patches CoreGraphics zero-day exploited in targeted attacks

2026-09-29 · 3 outlets · full

Apple patched a CoreGraphics zero-day vulnerability, spotted by Meta, that had been exploited in extremely sophisticated targeted attacks against specific individuals running iOS 26. The flaw could let attackers achieve arbitrary code execution via a maliciously crafted file, and Apple is urging users still on iOS 26 to update.
