October Patch Tuesday fixes draw differing bug and zero-day counts across outlets

2026-10-13 · 2 outlets · conflicting

Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday shipped cumulative updates for Windows 11 and an extended security update for Windows 10, also marking Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro's end of support and the first official Windows 11 26H2 update.
