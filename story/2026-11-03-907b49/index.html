Apple rolls out Siri AI integration with third-party apps in iOS 27.3

2026-11-03 · 2 outlets · partial

Apple has begun letting Siri AI perform actions inside third-party apps as part of the iOS 27.3 rollout, giving developers access to features like personal context and on-screen awareness. The Register notes Apple first promised this kind of third-party Siri integration more than two years earlier, in 2024.
