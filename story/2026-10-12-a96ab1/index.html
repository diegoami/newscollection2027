Cyberattack disabled refrigeration at hundreds of French supermarkets, spoiling food

2026-10-12 · 4 outlets · full

A cyberattack remotely defrosted refrigeration systems at hundreds of French supermarkets by targeting Danfoss controllers running outdated firmware, according to the manufacturer. Temperatures soared to 50C overnight, spoiling stock; one store reported fridge temperatures reaching 27C (80F) and a total loss of fresh and frozen food.
