United Airlines blocks AI agents from booking and managing flights

2026-11-11 · 4 outlets · partial

United Airlines said it will not let AI agents or personal assistants book flights or manage reservations, citing security, privacy and regulatory concerns, including possible DOT and FAA rule violations. The airline said it hasn't yet seen many customers try this. One outlet frames the move as requiring human-only safety checks rather than citing specific regulations.
