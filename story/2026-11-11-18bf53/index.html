United Airlines bars AI agents from booking or managing flights

2026-11-11 · 3 outlets · partial

United Airlines says it will not let AI agents or personal AI assistants book flights or manage reservations, citing security, cybersecurity and regulatory concerns, including possible DOT and FAA rule violations. One report notes United hasn't yet seen many people try using AI agents to book flights.
