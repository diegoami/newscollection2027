Hobbyists build Cray-1 supercomputer replica from 30 vintage Mac Minis

2026-10-09 · 2 outlets · full

A working replica of the legendary 1975 Cray-1 supercomputer has been built from 30 vintage, 'obsolete' Mac Minis. Ars Technica reports the build is displayed at Spain's Museo de Historia de la Computación as a piece of retro-styled upcycling, while Tom's Hardware reports the replica delivers 1.5 TFLOPS of compute, calling it a tribute showcasing decades of computing innovation.
