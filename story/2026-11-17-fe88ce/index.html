California bans Waymo from freeways; Waymo pauses rides and issues recall

2026-11-17 · 7 outlets · full

California's DMV banned Waymo's self-driving cars from state freeways, calling their freeway operations an imminent hazard after a series of incidents, including a driverless car passing a slowing Highway Patrol vehicle. Waymo suspended freeway rides nationwide and will stay off highways until it shows effective corrective actions. Robotaxis had already been recalled once in May, and Waymo issued a new recall after vehicles drove into closed lanes.
