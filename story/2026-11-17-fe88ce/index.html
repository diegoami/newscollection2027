Waymo suspends freeway robotaxi rides after California DMV order

2026-11-17 · 6 outlets · full

California's DMV ordered Waymo to stop freeway robotaxi rides nationwide, saying the operations pose an imminent hazard to public safety; restrictions stay until Waymo shows effective corrective action. The order follows incidents including a Waymo trying to pass a Highway Patrol vehicle, and an earlier May recall over construction-zone incursions.
