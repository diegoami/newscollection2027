Waymo pulls robotaxis from US freeways after California safety ban

2026-11-17 · 7 outlets · partial

California's DMV banned Waymo's driverless cars from state freeways, citing an imminent hazard to public safety, after incidents including one vehicle trying to pass a Highway Patrol car slowing traffic. Waymo responded by suspending freeway rides nationwide while it works on corrective actions. Coverage differs on which specific incident is cited as the immediate cause, with some outlets also noting a related recall in May over construction-zone incursions.
