ShinyHunters says it breached FBI, stealing agents personal data

2026-09-22 · 6 outlets · partial

Hacking group ShinyHunters claims it breached FBI systems via an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day, stealing agents' and applicants' personal data, including names, badge numbers, addresses and medical test results. The hackers say the attack was not financially motivated. The FBI is investigating, and agents fear blackmail or foreign counterintelligence risk. One report says the bureau still had not confirmed a breach even as other coverage already described it as a confirmed hack.
