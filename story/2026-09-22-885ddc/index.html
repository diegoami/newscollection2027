ShinyHunters claims a major FBI data breach via PeopleSoft flaw

2026-09-22 · 6 outlets · partial

The hacking group ShinyHunters claims it breached FBI systems through an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day, stealing personal data on agents and applicants, including names, badge numbers, addresses and even blood and urine test results. The FBI is investigating whether the claims, including reports of up to 3TB of data, are real. ShinyHunters says the hack was not financially motivated, though BleepingComputer describes the group as an extortion gang.
