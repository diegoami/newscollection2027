North Korea blamed for record Binance crypto heist as withdrawals resume

2026-10-29 · 10 outlets · conflicting

Binance suffered a major cryptocurrency theft, with outlets giving differing dollar estimates over time. Investigators linked the breach to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop and attributed it to North Korean hackers known as TraderTraitor, which the FBI later confirmed. Binance froze withdrawals, its CEO said 90 percent of stolen funds had been replenished, and the exchange then resumed withdrawals after covering 95 percent of the losses.
