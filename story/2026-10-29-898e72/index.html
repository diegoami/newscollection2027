Binance hack blamed on North Korea; initial loss estimates vary, Pyongyang denies involvement

2026-10-29 · 10 outlets · conflicting

Binance was hacked for an estimated $6.3 billion in what outlets call the largest crypto theft on record, with early reports ranging from $5.6 billion to $6.3 billion. North Korean state hackers, including a unit called TraderTraitor, were blamed and later named by the FBI, though North Korea denied involvement. Binance says the breach began with a fake job interview and malware that compromised an engineer's laptop, and the exchange has since replenished most of the stolen funds.
