Binance hit by record multibillion-dollar crypto hack, North Korea blamed

2026-10-29 · 10 outlets · partial

Binance suffered a major cryptocurrency theft, with outlets initially reporting figures between roughly $5.6 billion and $6.3 billion. Analytics firms and outlets link the breach to North Korean hackers, reportedly via a compromised employee laptop, with one report saying the theft took only 20 minutes. Binance moved to freeze affected assets, recovering only part of the funds at first, and its CEO later said 90 percent of the stolen assets had been replenished, with the company covering the rest.
