Binance hack losses estimated $5.6bn to $6.3bn, blamed on North Korea

2026-10-29 · 10 outlets · conflicting

Binance suffered what multiple outlets called the largest crypto heist on record, with early loss estimates ranging from $5.6 billion to $6.3 billion. The breach was linked to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, and trackers and later the FBI attributed it to North Korean hackers known as TraderTraitor. Binance froze and later resumed withdrawals, pledged to cover losses, and reported recovering most of the funds as investors pulled billions from the exchange.
