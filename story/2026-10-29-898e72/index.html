Binance hack estimated at $5.6-6.3 billion, blamed on North Korean hackers

2026-10-29 · 9 outlets · conflicting

Outlets reported a major Binance crypto hack, with early estimates of $5.8 billion rising to a widely repeated $6.3 billion figure, while Tom's Hardware put the loss at $5.6 billion. Investigators and multiple outlets linked the theft to a DevOps engineer's compromised laptop and attributed it to North Korean hackers, including the group TraderTraitor. Binance said it would cover losses and moved to freeze stolen assets, though only part of the total had been recovered as of these reports.
