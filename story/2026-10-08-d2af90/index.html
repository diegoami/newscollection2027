Anthropic bans cruel or abusive treatment of Claude in policy update

2026-10-08 · 6 outlets · partial

Anthropic updated its usage policy to ban 'sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior' toward Claude, its first such change in over a year. The rules also target election interference, weapons development and surveillance, while still permitting ordinary criticism. Anthropic has not detailed what counts as abusive content. Most outlets describe the change as a ban, though one frames it as merely asking users to stop.
