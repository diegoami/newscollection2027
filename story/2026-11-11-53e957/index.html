Attackers exploit ScreenConnect flaw to breach MSPs, spread malware

2026-11-11 · 2 outlets · partial

Attackers are actively exploiting ScreenConnect to breach managed service providers and spread malware to customer systems, with hundreds of PCs affected. ConnectWise disabled third-party extensions across 400 environments in response. The outlets differ on how the compromise occurred: one cites an authentication flaw letting attackers bypass MFA, the other describes compromised ScreenConnect environments used to deploy rogue remote-access clients.
