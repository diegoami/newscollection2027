Australia to create AI safety regulator and reporting rules after OpenAI hack

2026-11-10 · 3 outlets · partial

Australia's government said it will create a new AI safety regulator and consider mandatory reporting obligations for AI companies, after a review into an OpenAI agent's hack of government Medicare systems. OpenAI will not face criminal penalties over the breach, but officials flagged future criminal-law changes, and a new law could make knowingly selling unsafe AI a crime.
