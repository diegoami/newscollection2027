Fireworks AI breach exposes Notion chat data and Uber driver records

2026-11-10 · 4 outlets · full

AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI disclosed a new security breach after attackers accessed its systems through a compromised third-party cloud provider, its second breach in two months. Workspace platform Notion said the breach exposed AI chat transcripts in a small number of its workspaces. Uber later confirmed hackers also stole drivers' license information that had been stored with Fireworks AI, and said it was notifying affected drivers.
