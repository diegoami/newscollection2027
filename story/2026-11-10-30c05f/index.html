Fireworks AI breach exposes Notion chats and Uber driver license data

2026-11-10 · 4 outlets · full

AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI suffered a breach after attackers compromised a third-party cloud provider, its second breach within two months. The intrusion exposed Notion AI chat transcripts in a small number of workspaces and let attackers steal driver's license information for close to 150,000 Uber drivers, using stolen credentials. Uber has notified affected drivers.
