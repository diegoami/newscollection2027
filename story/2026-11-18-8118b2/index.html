GTA VI targets 30 fps on consoles, with lower resolution on Series S

2026-11-18 · 2 outlets · full

GTA VI will target 30 frames per second on PlayStation and Xbox consoles, with no 60 fps performance mode, now or in future patches. On PS5 and Xbox Series X the game runs at 1440p; Xbox Series S is limited to 720p with upscaling. Different anti-aliasing and upscaling technologies are used per platform, including Sony's PSSR 2 and AMD's FSR 4. Reports note the developer has a history of shipping games at lower framerates than competitors.
