Google to launch experimental orbital AI data center Oct 1

2026-09-24 · 4 outlets · full

Google plans to launch an experimental orbital AI data center satellite on October 1 as part of Project Suncatcher. The satellite will carry four TPUs and 1,000W of solar power, running for only 15 minutes at a time in a proof of concept testing how well lightly modified compute performs in orbit.
