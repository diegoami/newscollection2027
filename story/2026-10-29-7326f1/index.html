Reddit to require login for all users as AI search cuts traffic

2026-10-29 · 4 outlets · partial

Reddit is ending logged-out browsing and will require all users to log in, outlets report. The company links the move to declining referral traffic as AI-powered search tools change how people find information online. Reddit CEO Steve Huffman raised the traffic decline on the company's Q3 2026 earnings call, and the login requirement will begin with tests outside the United States.
