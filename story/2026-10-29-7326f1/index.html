Reddit to require logins as AI search cuts referral traffic

2026-10-29 · 5 outlets · partial

Reddit is moving toward a logged-in-only experience, ending the ability to browse the site without an account. Outlets largely attribute the shift to declining organic search traffic, which CEO Steve Huffman said AI-powered search tools such as Google's AI Mode do not replace. One outlet instead frames the change as a move to block scrapers from the site's content. The rollout is reportedly starting with tests outside the US.
