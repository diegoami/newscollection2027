Discord will let search engines index public server messages

2026-10-27 · 4 outlets · full

Discord is making messages from its public Discoverable Servers indexable by search engines, so people outside the platform can find and view them without an account or invitation. Users will be able to opt out. TechCrunch says the change begins in December; some users have raised concerns about it.
