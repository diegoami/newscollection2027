New York says T-Mobile illegally capped low-income home internet speeds

2026-11-11 · 2 outlets · full

New York says T-Mobile illegally imposed a 50 GB data cap on a low-income home internet plan required to offer 25Mbps speeds, throttling connections to 600Kbps once the cap was hit. The state is forcing T-Mobile to stop applying data caps to plans offered under the Affordable Broadband Act.
