South Korean bank hack blamed on Chinese actor using Claude agents, ARTEX AI tool

2026-10-05 · 3 outlets · partial

South Korea's Financial Services Commission investigated a wave of cyberattacks on banks after the country's president said there were signs hackers used AI, with the breach exposing data from about 25,000 customers. Investigators later attributed the attacks to a Chinese-speaking actor using the Claude Code tool and the agentic ARTEX penetration-testing suite, and CrowdStrike reportedly found a CV possibly belonging to the hacker among exposed AI logs.
