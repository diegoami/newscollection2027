FCC approves SpaceX plan for 1 million AI satellites

2026-11-25 · 6 outlets · partial

The FCC approved SpaceX's "Starmind" plan to deploy up to 1 million AI data-center satellites, dismissing opponents' concerns, including threats to scientific research, as outside its authority. The constellation would increase satellites in orbit roughly 50-fold. Google and Blue Origin are pursuing similar projects. Coverage ranges from skeptical to describing the stated efficiency goal.
