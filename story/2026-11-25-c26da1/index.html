FCC approves SpaceX plan for up to 1 million AI satellites

2026-11-25 · 3 outlets · full

The FCC authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites under its 'Starmind' initiative, the agency's first such approval amid competing plans from Google and Blue Origin. The constellation would increase satellites in orbit roughly 50-fold; the FCC said it dismissed opponents' concerns, including about scientific research, as outside its regulatory authority.
