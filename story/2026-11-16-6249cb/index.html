Harvard study finds hyperscale data centers' power use rising fast

2026-11-16 · 5 outlets · full

A Harvard-led study in Nature Sustainability found that hyperscale US data centers consume roughly 2% of US electricity, with about 403 facilities using roughly 82 TWh in a year, more than Michigan's entire consumption. Fossil fuels remain the largest electricity source for these facilities, and the study found their attributable emissions are two to three times higher than in 2018.
