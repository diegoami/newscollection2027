Anduril and Qualcomm launch BlueChip to build laptops in the US

2026-10-27 · 3 outlets · partial

Defense contractor Anduril and chipmaker Qualcomm announced a partnership called BlueChip to build laptops in the United States, starting with a single model in 2027. Anduril founder Palmer Luckey says the goal is to manufacture every individual component domestically, including parts currently made only in China, though early prototypes draw about 90% of procurement costs from US and allied suppliers. The companies describe full domestic sourcing as a long-term goal.
