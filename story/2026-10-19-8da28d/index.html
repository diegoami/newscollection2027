Amazon to fund new nuclear plant at former Indiana coal site

2026-10-19 · 3 outlets · partial

Amazon signed a deal with X-energy, and AEP according to one report, to fund a nuclear power facility replacing a defunct coal plant in Indiana, expected to generate up to 960 megawatts, or nearly a gigawatt. One report specifies 12 small reactors, while another notes X-energy has yet to build a working reactor.
