Amazon ends NDAs for data centers but still faces community backlash

2026-10-02 · 3 outlets · partial

Amazon says it has stopped using NDAs with county officials over data centers, part of a $1 billion plan to address community backlash that has led to moratoriums in some areas. Ars Technica reports the move drew praise for ending NDAs but criticism for downplaying pollution, while Amazon Web Services' CEO pushed back on what he called widespread suspicion of data centers.
