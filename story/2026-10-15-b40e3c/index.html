2026-10-15 · 6 outlets · partial

Assessments varied: one reviewer found it nearly dethrones Apple but for battery life, another said it mostly delivers on Microsoft's promise of one machine for development, AI, and gaming, and a third said the Windows experience is largely unchanged, with RTX Spark benefiting mainly AI enthusiasts.
