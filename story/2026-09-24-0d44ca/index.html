Google adds lifelike Live Avatar feature to Gemini 3.8

2026-09-24 · 3 outlets · partial

Google has introduced a 'Live Avatar' feature for Gemini 3.8 Live, letting an animated AI persona lip-sync and show facial expressions in real time, choosing between cartoon or more lifelike styles. The feature, aimed at customer service and other tasks, is currently limited to Gemini Enterprise customers. Engadget describes the avatars as 'creepy,' while other outlets are more neutral.
