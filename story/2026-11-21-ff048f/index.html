UN talks on regulating autonomous weapons collapse amid Russian objections

2026-11-21 · 2 outlets · partial

Week-long UN talks in Geneva aimed at a negotiating mandate for a binding treaty on autonomous weapons, or "killer robots," ended without agreement after Russian objections, despite support from an overwhelming majority of states at the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons. One outlet described the outcome as a collapse, while another reported delegates agreed only to start discussing options going forward.
