OpenAI launches always-on 'Dots' agents to rival Meta's Muse

2026-09-29 · 6 outlets · partial

At its DevDay keynote in San Francisco, OpenAI unveiled Dots, always-on AI agents powered by its GPT-6 Astra model that work across connected apps in the background, positioned as an answer to Meta's Muse. The launch came less than a day after OpenAI said it was scrapping a new model over safety concerns; one report described Dots as a rebrand of OpenAI's agents rather than a new launch.
