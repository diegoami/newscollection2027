OpenAI launches Dots, an always-on AI agent rivaling Meta's Muse

2026-09-29 · 6 outlets · partial

OpenAI unveiled Dots, an always-on AI agent platform powered by its GPT-6 Astra model, at its DevDay event, positioning it against Meta's Muse. CEO Sam Altman also said OpenAI won't go public until its models are safe. A later hands-on report describes Dots as a business-first product costing at least $100 a month, while other outlets differ on tone: some call it cute and disarming, others say it feels like enterprise workplace software rather than approachable like Muse.
