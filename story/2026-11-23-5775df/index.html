Army detonates lead azide stockpile in Iowa; weight estimates vary

2026-11-23 · 2 outlets · conflicting

The US Army conducted a controlled detonation of its lead azide stockpile at an Iowa ammunition plant to prevent a possible spontaneous explosion, after local officials said they learned details mainly from a press release. The EPA later reported 'zero' airborne lead. Outlets gave different figures for the stockpile's weight, ranging from 9,500 to nearly 10,000 pounds.
