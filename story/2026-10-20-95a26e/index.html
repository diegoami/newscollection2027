Weak eMudhra CA private keys let attackers impersonate websites, browsers respond

2026-10-20 · 3 outlets · partial

Researchers found eMudhra's private keys for eight CA certificates were so weak they could be recovered and used to impersonate any website; major browsers blocked the compromised certificates. eMudhra says it passed audits and calls the issue a minor error, at odds with the severity described elsewhere.
