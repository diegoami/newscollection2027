ULA's Vulcan rocket fails, destroying 40 Amazon satellites, a first

2026-11-15 · 5 outlets · full

United Launch Alliance's Vulcan rocket failed during launch, destroying 40 of Amazon's Leo broadband satellites in what the company called its first total mission failure in 20 years. ULA later said the failure was caused by inadequate fuel supply to the engines, after earlier reporting suggested the BE-4 engines might not be at fault.
