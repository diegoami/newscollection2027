Japan launches MMX mission to retrieve samples from Mars moon Phobos

2026-10-19 · 2 outlets · full

Japan's JAXA-led MMX mission has launched on an international effort to bring soil samples back to Earth from Phobos, one of Mars' moons. The mission's climax is expected in 2029, when the spacecraft will attempt up to two landings on Phobos, with a successful return potentially revealing new clues about the solar system's history.
