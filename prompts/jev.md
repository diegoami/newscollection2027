Do article_a and article_b report the same event?

Same event means one identifiable announcement, launch, filing, lawsuit, ruling, funding round, resignation, outage, breach or incident, that both articles report. A problem and the response to it (a bug and its patch, an outage and its restoration, a breach and its disclosure) count as the same event.

Not the same event: articles that only share a subject, company or product but report different events; a launch and a later review of the product; the same kind of event at different companies; a roundup, opinion piece, deal post or newsletter that merely mentions the event the other reports.

Answer yes only if the titles and ledes make it clear. If you cannot tell, answer no.
