Tampered Ledger hardware wallets sold through reseller drain users' crypto

2026-10-10 · 3 outlets · full

Ledger confirmed that hardware crypto wallets distributed through a Southeast Asian reseller were compromised with an implanted chip that drained users' cryptocurrency. The implants used eSIM and LTE modules to steal recovery phrases, with more than $71 million stolen so far in an ongoing supply-chain attack; Ledger asked the reseller to pause sales while it investigates.

Nadella warns all AI models should be treated as compromised

2026-10-10 · 2 outlets · full

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella used a lengthy Saturday post on X to argue that AI models should be assumed compromised and that the industry needs an 'emergency brake.' He said it is time to reassess AI's 'trust architecture' rather than treat AI systems as a 'set of nested black boxes' whose outputs are simply accepted.
