Ledger reviews policies after supply-chain hardware implant drains crypto wallets

2026-10-10 · 3 outlets · full

Ledger hardware wallets sold through a third-party reseller were fitted with hidden implants that captured recovery phrases and drained funds. Ledger asked the reseller to pause sales during its investigation and is reviewing its chain-of-custody and anti-tamper policies. Tom's Hardware reported the estimated stolen total grew from $71 million to $93 million as coverage continued.

Nadella warns all AI models should be treated as compromised

2026-10-10 · 2 outlets · full

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella used a lengthy Saturday post on X to argue that AI models should be assumed compromised and that the industry needs an 'emergency brake.'
