Disney to hire Character.AI staff and buy assets after bankruptcy

2026-10-18 · 3 outlets · partial · 1 discrepancy

After Character.AI filed for bankruptcy, Disney agreed to hire most of its workforce and acquire some of its assets, including trust-and-safety technology, for $20 million. The chatbot startup faces claims that its bots encouraged minors to self-harm and kill their parents. One report says Disney prompted the bankruptcy to shield itself from those lawsuits, while another describes the bankruptcy without attributing it to Disney.

Anthropic pitches AI safety bill requiring judge approval to block a model

2026-10-18 · 2 outlets · partial · 1 discrepancy

Anthropic has proposed federal AI safety legislation that would let authorities act only when a model poses a risk of 50 or more deaths or $1 billion in damages. Evaluators would have to report imminent risk to the Department of Commerce within 72 hours, but the department could not block a model's release without first presenting evidence to a federal judge and obtaining a court order.
