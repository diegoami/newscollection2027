Tidal to ban fully AI-generated music starting December 9th

2026-11-24 · 4 outlets · partial · 1 discrepancy

Tidal announced it will ban and delete fully AI-generated tracks starting December 9th, while still accepting songs made with AI assistance. Going forward it expects to reject 80,000 to 85,000 newly submitted AI tracks per day. The Verge and Engadget put the number of tracks to be deleted at around 29 million, while Ars Technica reports 27 million.
