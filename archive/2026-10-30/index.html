Malicious Claude Code extension on Cursor exposed thousands of developers

2026-10-30 · 4 outlets · partial · 1 discrepancy

BleepingComputer, Wired and Ars Technica report a malicious version of the Claude Code VS Code extension was distributed through Cursor's marketplace, exposing thousands of developers to SSH-backdoor malware, for roughly 11 to 12 hours depending on the outlet. Wired says flawed code in the malware limited the damage, while The Register and Ars Technica report Anthropic denies responsibility and attributes the compromise to a malicious actor who accessed its verified Cursor account.

Waymo to use Toyota bZ4X vehicles for robotaxi service in Japan

2026-10-30 · 3 outlets · partial · 1 discrepancy

Wikimedia stakes out middle ground in AI copyright cases

2026-10-30 · 2 outlets · full

The Verge and Engadget report the Wikimedia Foundation is weighing in on landmark AI copyright cases, including the NYT's case against OpenAI. It is pushing courts to protect LLMs training on copyrighted material while not shielding chatbots that regurgitate that work to users, warning that an extreme ruling in either direction could threaten the open web.
